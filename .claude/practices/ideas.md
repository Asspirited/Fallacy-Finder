## Promotion Criteria

An idea moves from UNREVIEWED to backlog when:
- It has a clear "so that" clause (a named user or product outcome)
- It has had at least an informal Three Amigos
- It is session-sized (one Gherkin → red → green → clean → push), or has been decomposed

If any of these are missing, leave in UNREVIEWED. Do not force it.
